Jump Lab

Camera setup

Get the phone right before you record. Marking the jump starts after you close this.

  1. Side-on

    Sagittal view. The camera looks across the jump, not from the front.

  2. Tripod height

    About hip height, or level with the top of the box.

  3. Distance

    About 3–4 m away.

  4. Square to the plane

    Lens perpendicular to the plane of the jump.

  5. 1× lens

    No digital zoom.

  6. Full frame

    Whole body, the box, and the landing area stay in frame.

  7. Light

    Bright and even. Avoid shooting into a window.

  8. 240 fps

    iPhone slo-mo. The capture frame rate starts at 240.

The clip stays on this phone. A saved jump stores frames and numbers only.
